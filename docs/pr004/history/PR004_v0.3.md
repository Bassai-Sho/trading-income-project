## 1. Question and role

Unchanged from v0.2. Owner goals: (1) income/wealth and (3) learn/build capability, with (2) diversification secondary. Paper-first. Days-scale rule (~3–4 day holds); a weeks-to-months trend-following family is a separate item, **PR-005, to be drafted and hashed before the G2 session runs** (not merely "before this item unblinds" — pinned to a concrete build step, §8).

**Stated prior, unchanged:** the published post-2008 result is ~1.35%/yr on capital, price-only, no costs. Even if it replicates, it likely doesn't matter for goal (1). Value is mostly learning, plus a possible small diversifier.

## 2. Hypotheses

**H0 — replication.** Our implementation reproduces the published figures within the bands in §7 (G1b), under the pinned conventions in §0.2.

**H1 — mechanism, all days.** Within days above the 200-day average, a lower RSI(2) predicts a higher forward 3-day return, controlling for volatility state. Primary endpoint: the coefficient on E3 (2016–2022).

**H2 — the rule as a sleeve (report, not gated).**

## 3. Rules

Unchanged from v0.2. **Primary P1:** close > SMA200 and RSI2 < 5 → buy at the close; exit when close > SMA5. Long only, one position, no stops, 1× notional, fixed capital (no compounding). Ties: strict inequalities, 1e-9 tolerance. Stage R: signal-close fills. Stage V: next-open entry **and** exit. **V2:** threshold 10. **V3:** short extension (< SMA200, RSI2 > 95, exit < SMA5), reported by side. Excluded: cumulative-RSI, IBS, VIX filter, stops, RSI-exit variants, optimisation.

## 6. Costs and the G2 equation

**Costs unchanged from v0.2** (two accounting modes, DFF daily rate, 2 bps/side, 3× after a >2σ prior-day move [ASSUMED]).

**G2, pinned as an equation** (fixed at freeze, not adjusted after seeing E3):

```
r3(t) = a + b * RSI2(t) + c * vol20(t) + d * absret1(t) + e(t)
```
- `r3(t)`: price-only close-to-close return from day t to t+3 (ex-dividend; a total-return sensitivity is reported, not gated, to match G1b's price-only convention).
- `RSI2(t)`: as defined in §3. **Expected sign of b: negative** (lower RSI2 → higher forward return).
- `vol20(t)`: standard deviation of daily log returns over the 20 trading days *before* t (not including t).
- `absret1(t)`: |close-to-close return on day t−1 to t|.
- Winsorize `r3` and `absret1` at **±15%** (fixed constant, reused from G0 — never a sample quantile, which would use future information).
- Sample: days with close(t) > SMA200(t), era E3 only for the primary test; E1 and E2 run the same equation separately and reported (not gated) for decay context.
- Standard errors: Newey–West, lag 5 (exceeds the 3-day return overlap).
- **MDE is computed and printed from the actual E1+E2 data before E3 is read**, so INCONCLUSIVE is interpretable rather than a shrug. [An order-of-magnitude estimate from a back-of-envelope calculation during review was roughly 5–10 bps per RSI point; the script computes the real number.]

**CONTRADICTED (redefined):** E3's one-sided 95% CI upper bound sits below the **pooled E1+E2 coefficient's lower bound** (bound-vs-bound, not CI-vs-point-estimate — a point estimate has no width to compare against).

## 7. Gates and steps, in order

- **G0** Data integrity (§4). Failure stops the item.
- **G1 Implementation.** Two independently coded versions (one vectorised, one an explicit day-by-day loop) agree on 100% of position-days, trades and daily P&L under the tie policy, plus the owner's manual audit of 10 random episodes.
- **G1b Replication (H0).** Exact quote this gate reproduces: *"I tested this strategy for myself using Amibroker and historical data from Norgate between 1/1/1995 and 1/1/2008 (without any transaction costs) and I achieved... No. of trades = 49, No. of winners = 83.7%... Annualised return [not given for E1]"* and *"tested the strategy between 1/1/2008 and 1/1/2016... No. of trades = 30, No. of Winners = 80%... Annualised return = 1.35%, Maximum drawdown = -13.19%."* Bands: E1 trades 45–53, win rate 78–89%; E2 trades 26–34, win rate 72–88%, return 0.5–2.5%, max drawdown −9% to −17% [ASSUMED bands around the exact quoted figures]. **Convention: price-only, costless, flat capital earns 0%** for this gate specifically (matching the source; the main analysis elsewhere uses the cash-rate convention in §6). **Failure protocol:** one documented investigation (data first, then implementation), at most one fix-and-rerun; an unattributable miss closes the item. E3 is not read until this gate passes.
- **Event-driven replay rehearsal** (before G7, after G1b passes): run the frozen pipeline day-by-day on the project's own 2016–2024 1-minute SPY store with next-open fills, as a dress rehearsal for both Stage V and forward paper trading. Tests the off-by-one and fill-timing failure class that G0's return-correlation check cannot see.
- **G2 Mechanism (H1),** as pinned in §6, on E3. Also reported on E1 and E2 for decay context, and as a bucket table (RSI < 5, 5–10, 10–30, 30–70, > 70) with an episode-level bootstrap CI.
- **G3 Nulls**, evaluated only if E3 has ≥ 30 entries (otherwise "not evaluable — power"): sign-flip (coding check only); vol-matched random entry; down-move-matched random entry.
- **G4 Economic report** (not gated): net CAGR, vol, Sharpe, Jensen alpha with CI, max drawdown, time in market, both cost modes; **blend test at 25% / 50% / 100% sleeve allocation** into a 100% SPY book; long-book loss on SPY's 20 worst days and short-book loss on its 20 worst up-days (V3 only); the mean close→next-open "gap wedge" on entry days (how much of the published edge Stage V's execution eats); top-3-episode P&L share (labelled "episode-dependent" above 60%); year table; volatility-tercile and long/short breakdowns; full trade list as an audit appendix.
- **G7 (E4, once):** single named, hashed script; report only, in the frozen template.

**Verdict categories (fixed now):** CONFIRMED / CONTRADICTED (§6) / INCONCLUSIVE (power) — MDE stated alongside whichever applies.
