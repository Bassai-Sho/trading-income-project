## 6. Costs and the G2 equation — CONTRADICTED corrected

Costs and the regression equation unchanged from v0.3 (§6 there). **Verdict logic, corrected:**

Let `b̂3, se3` be E3's coefficient and standard error; `b̂12, se12` the same, pooled over E1+E2 (expected sign of both: negative).

```
z = (b̂3 − b̂12) / sqrt(se3² + se12²)
CONTRADICTED  iff  z > 1.645     (E3's coefficient significantly WEAKER — less negative — than published)
CONFIRMED     iff  NOT CONTRADICTED  and  (b̂3 + 1.645·se3) < 0     (evaluated in that order — precedence matters)
INCONCLUSIVE  otherwise
```

**Verified on four cases before adopting** (b12=−8, se12=2, se3=4, illustrative units):

| E3 coefficient | Reading | Old (v0.3) definition | New definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (dead) | mechanism gone | CONTRADICTED=False, CONFIRMED=False → *proceeds to paper trading* | **CONTRADICTED=True** |
| −20 (much stronger) | should never read as "contradicted" | CONTRADICTED=**True** and CONFIRMED=True simultaneously | CONTRADICTED=False, CONFIRMED=True |
| −7 (basically unchanged) | should confirm | — | CONFIRMED=True |
| −2 (mostly decayed, not dead) | genuinely ambiguous | — | **INCONCLUSIVE** (correctly ambiguous, not forced either way) |

**Pre-written interpretation sentence for the INCONCLUSIVE branch** (so the report can't slide between "no power" and "consistent with decay" after the fact): *"If MDE (computed from E1+E2, printed before E3 is read) exceeds the observed |b̂3|'s confidence interval width, the result is under-powered; if b̂3's point estimate sits between zero and b̂12 without either bound being decisive, the honest reading is partial decay, not absence of a mechanism."*

**Pre-written bucket-interpretation sentence:** *"CONFIRMED is read as 'the mechanism operates in the rule's own tail' only if the RSI<5 and RSI<10 buckets individually show the same sign as the pooled regression; if the regression coefficient is driven by mid-range variation instead, the report says so explicitly rather than letting a global coefficient imply tail-region skill."*
