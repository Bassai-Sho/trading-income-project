## 1. Question and role

Owner goals: (1) expected income/wealth and (3) learn/build capability, with (2) diversification secondary. Paper-first: nothing trades real money until proven on paper.

This item tests the owner's "swing" idea in its **days-scale** form: does short-term index mean reversion carry a timing edge that survives realistic costs and beats simply holding the index? Holds are ~3–5 days, not weeks or months; a weeks-to-months family (trend following) is a separate candidate, not this item. Time-box: the project review on 21 Nov 2026, at roughly 10 hours a week.

## 2. Hypotheses (the primary result needs both)

**H1 — timing skill, free of market beta.** After an entry signal, the forward 3-day return exceeds the average forward 3-day return over all days in the *same 200-day-trend state* (above or below the average). k = 1 and 5 are reported. *Why this baseline:* the long signal only fires above the 200-day average, where average returns differ from the all-days average; comparing with all days would credit the trend filter's return to the RSI timing.

**H2 — investability.** Net of the costs in section 6, the rule at 1× notional (a) beats buy-and-hold on Sharpe with a maximum drawdown no worse, and (b) does not add loss on the market's worst days (gate G5).

## 3. Rules

**Primary, V1** — the "widely published" Connors set. *Exact numbers vary between secondary sources, and none has been checked against the 2008 book* **[UNVERIFIED]**.

- RSI(2): Wilder smoothing on daily closes, seeded with the simple average of the first two changes.
- Trend: 200-day simple moving average (SMA200) of the close. Fast exit line: SMA5.
- **Long** entry: close > SMA200 and RSI2 < 10. Long exit: close > SMA5.
- **Short** entry: close < SMA200 and RSI2 > 90. Short exit: close < SMA5.
- One position at a time. No stops (as published). Flat otherwise. 1× notional. Signals are computed on the day's close.

**Execution, two runs (the PR-003 pattern).** *Stage R (fidelity):* trade at the signal day's close, as published. *Stage V (viability):* trade at the **next day's open**.

**Declared secondary variants**, reported as a family; each counts as a trial in the DSR:
- **V2:** entry thresholds 5 / 95.
- **V3:** exit on RSI2 > 65 (long) or < 35 (short) instead of the SMA5 cross.

**Deliberately excluded to control multiplicity** (each could become its own item): the cumulative-RSI variant (the book's reported SPY test), IBS, any VIX filter, stops, parameter optimisation.

## 4. Data and integrity (gate G0)

**Primary:** SPY daily open/high/low/close, *unadjusted*, plus dividends, 1993-01-29 to 2024-12-31, from yfinance (an unofficial, single source). **Secondary, reported but not gated:** QQQ from 1999-03-10.

**G0 cross-check** on 2016-01-04 to 2024-12-31 against the project's own SIP-derived daily bars (last 1-minute close, raw prices):

## 6. Costs and accounting **[ASSUMED — for review]**

- **Transaction:** 2 bps of notional per side (spread plus slippage; conservative for either MES futures or a spread bet). Sensitivity at 1 and 4 bps.
- **Financing:** (FEDFUNDS + 1.0%) / 365 per calendar day on notional, charged on longs *and* shorts, with the rate floored at 0. FEDFUNDS is monthly, from FRED (to be downloaded; the project's store starts 2016).
- **Dividends:** a long receives, a short pays, at actual SPY amounts.
- **Flat days earn the cash rate.** Sharpe uses excess-over-cash returns for both the rule and buy-and-hold. Buy-and-hold is total return: (close + dividend) / previous close − 1.
- Vehicle-neutral by design; the owner's vehicle is undecided.

## 7. Gates (in order; failing any one fails PR-004; no rescue variants)

- **G1 Implementation.** An independently coded second implementation agrees with the first on **100%** of signal days on E1–E3. On E1 the long trades' win rate is ≥ 70% **[ASSUMED sanity band; sources report roughly 75–85% for the book's variants, unverified]**. This is a sanity check on the code, not a hypothesis test.
- **G2 (H1).** Mean excess forward 3-day return after entry signals is > 0, with a one-sided 95% block-bootstrap lower bound > 0, on E2+E3. Entries are the first day of each signal episode; block length ≥ 5.
- **G3 Nulls** (1,000 draws each, p < 0.05): (a) *sign-flip*: same timing, opposite side; (b) *regime-matched random entry*: same number of entries and hold-length distribution, entries drawn at random from days in the same trend state.
- **G4 Viability** (Stage V: next open, with costs). Net Sharpe ≥ buy-and-hold Sharpe on E2+E3 **and** max drawdown ≤ buy-and-hold's; deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95 with N = 27 (carried forward) + 3 (V1–V3). *Pre-declared classification:* if ≥ 70% of position-days are long, the buy-and-hold hurdle above applies; otherwise the benchmark is cash and correlation with SPY must be ≤ 0.3.
- **G5 Co-crash.** On SPY's 20 worst days in E2+E3, the rule's summed P&L is ≥ −25% of buy-and-hold's summed P&L on the same days **[ASSUMED threshold]**, i.e. it does not double down on the owner's existing long exposure.
- **G6 Stability.** Net positive in ≥ 10 of the 15 calendar years of E2+E3 **[ASSUMED]**. Crash windows are reported separately: 2008-09 to 2009-03, 2011-08, 2015-08, 2018-Q4, 2020-Q1, 2022.
- **G7 (E4, once).** Net Sharpe ≥ 50% of the E2+E3 net Sharpe, and the H1 sign holds.
- **Then:** forward paper trading on the live data store (new evidence, per the owner's rule).

## 9. Known weak points (please attack these)

1. "The published rule" is ambiguous: sources disagree on the exit (SMA5 cross vs RSI > 65 vs RSI > 70 with a 10-day cap) and on entry confirmations, and the book's own SPY test reportedly used a cumulative-RSI variant. No primary-source check.
2. Every threshold marked **[ASSUMED]** is judgement, not derived.
3. The rule is widely known, so post-publication decay is expected.
4. One instrument, and low power: a blog test reports 181 long trades over 26 years **[UNVERIFIED]**, so on the order of 100 entries in E2+E3.
5. The drawdown criterion is nearly guaranteed for a low-exposure rule; the binding tests are Sharpe, G2 and G3.
6. The analysts (human and AI) already know the general history of E2–E3; that contamination cannot be removed.
7. The data source is a single unofficial feed.
