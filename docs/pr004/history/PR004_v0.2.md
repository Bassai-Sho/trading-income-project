## 1. Question and role

Owner goals: (1) income/wealth and (3) learn/build capability, with (2) diversification secondary. Paper-first.

This item tests the owner's "swing" idea in its **days-scale** form, using the one index rule with a published test on the index. Holds are ~3–4 days, not weeks or months; a weeks-to-months trend-following family is a separate candidate (to be pre-registered as PR-005 before this item unblinds). **Whatever this item shows is evidence about one days-scale rule, not about "swing trading" as a family.**

**Prior stated up front:** the published post-2008 result for this rule is ~1.35% a year on capital (long-only index, no costs). Even if it replicates, it is unlikely to matter for goal (1). Its expected value is mostly learning, plus a possible small diversifier. Time-box: three build sessions, ending by the 21 Nov 2026 review.

## 2. Hypotheses

**H0 — replication (validity, not discovery).** Our implementation reproduces the published figures within the bands in G1b.

**H1 — mechanism, all days.** Within days above the 200-day average, a lower RSI(2) predicts a higher forward 3-day return, after controlling for volatility. *Primary endpoint: the RSI coefficient on 2016-01-01 to 2022-12-31.*

**H2 — the rule as a sleeve (report only).** Net of costs, what the frozen rule delivers alone and blended with holding SPY.

## 3. Rules

**Primary, P1** — the book's index test **[secondary source; UNVERIFIED against the 2008 book]**:
- RSI(2): Wilder smoothing on daily closes. Trend: SMA200 of the close. Exit line: SMA5.
- **Long only.** Entry: close > SMA200 **and RSI2 < 5**. Buy at the close. Exit when the close is above SMA5 (evaluated from the day *after* entry). One position at a time; no stops; flat otherwise.
- Ties: strict inequalities; a value within 1e-9 of a threshold does not trigger. Re-entry only after flat.
- Fixed 1× notional per position (no compounding). Sharpe uses daily excess-over-cash returns, annualised by √252.

**Execution.** *Stage R (fidelity):* trade at the signal close, as published. *Stage V (viability):* enter **and exit** at the next day's open.

**Declared secondary variants** (each counts as a trial): **V2:** threshold 10 instead of 5. **V3:** a symmetric short extension (close < SMA200, RSI2 > 95, exit close < SMA5), reported separately by side.

**Excluded to control multiplicity:** the cumulative-RSI SPY variant (the source's own replication of it failed: 127 trades against 50 published), IBS, any VIX filter, stops, RSI-based exits, parameter optimisation.

## 4. Data and integrity (gate G0)

**Primary:** SPY daily open/high/low/close, *unadjusted*, plus dividends, 1993-01-29 to 2024-12-31, yfinance (unofficial, single upstream). **Cross-checks:**
1. yfinance `^GSPC` index vs SPY×10 return correlation, 1993–2024 (catches errors in one series, not upstream-wide errors).
2. The project's own SIP-derived daily bars, 2016–2024: closes median ≤ 3 bps, p99 ≤ 10 bps; opens median ≤ 5 bps, p99 ≤ 20 bps **[ASSUMED]**; every exceedance individually reviewed and logged.
3. **The published figures themselves (G1b) act as an independent data check for 1995–2016** (they came from a different vendor). A mismatch means investigate the data first.
4. An optional second vendor for 2008–2015 (Stooq or Tiingo); availability **unverified**. FRED S&P 500 is **not usable** (10 years only).

**Asserts:** `Close` (not `Adj Close`) everywhere; no duplicate dates; every NYSE session present; |daily return| ≤ 15%; ≥ 200 prior rows before every evaluation day; dividend ex-dates equal the project's cached list for 2016–2024 (older amounts not independently verified — flagged).

## 6. Costs (assumed — for review)

Two modes, both reported, neither gated (the owner's vehicle is undecided). **Execution:** 2 bps per side (sensitivity 1 and 4 bps; Stage V uses 3× on days after a |return| > 2σ). **Cash rate:** DFF (daily effective fed funds, floored at 0).
- **Mode F (futures-style):** long P&L = total return − cash rate; short = −total return + cash rate.
- **Mode S (spread-bet-style):** longs pay DFF + 2.0%; shorts receive DFF − 2.0% (can be negative). *The 2.0% is a placeholder until the provider's published terms are sourced.*
- Flat days earn the cash rate. Buy-and-hold is total return: (close + dividend) / previous close − 1.

## 7. Tests and gates (in order)

- **G0** Data integrity (section 4). Failure stops the item.
- **G1 Implementation.** Two independently coded versions from the written spec (one vectorised, one an explicit loop) agree on 100% of position-days, trade lists and daily P&L under the tie policy. Plus a manual audit of 10 randomly chosen episodes in a spreadsheet by the owner.
- **G1b Replication (H0).** P1 on SPY, Stage R, over **1995-01-01 to 2007-12-31**: trades 45–53, win rate 80–88%, mean hold 3–4.5 days. Over **2008-01-01 to 2015-12-31**: trades 26–34, win rate 72–88%, annualised return on full capital 0.5–2.5%, max drawdown −9% to −17% **[ASSUMED bands around the published figures]**. Failure stops everything; E3 stays unread until it is resolved.
- **G2 Mechanism (H1), primary endpoint.** On E3, days above SMA200: forward 3-day return regressed on RSI(2), with controls for prior-20-day realised volatility and the prior day's absolute return; HAC standard errors (lag 5). Also mean forward returns by RSI bucket (<5, 5–10, 10–30, 30–70, >70) with an episode-level bootstrap CI, and the same regression on E1 and E2 separately to show decay.
- **G3 Nulls** for the rule's trades: sign-flip (a coding check only), plus **vol-matched** and **down-move-matched** random entry drawn from above-SMA200 days. Evaluated only if E3 has ≥ 30 entries; otherwise "not evaluable (power)".
- **G4 Economic report (not gated):** net CAGR, vol, Sharpe, Jensen alpha with CI, max drawdown, time in market, both cost modes; the **blend test** (does adding the rule at 1× improve the Sharpe and drawdown of a 100% SPY book); long-book loss on SPY's 20 worst days and (for V3) short-book loss on its 20 worst up-days; top-3-episode share of P&L (labelled "episode-dependent" above 60%); year table with trade counts; volatility-tercile and long/short breakdowns.
- **G7 (E4, once):** report only, in the frozen template.

**Verdict categories (fixed now):** *CONFIRMED* — H0 holds and the G2 coefficient's one-sided 95% CI excludes 0 with the expected sign on E3. *CONTRADICTED* — the CI's upper bound sits below the published-era effect. *INCONCLUSIVE (power)* — anything else.

**Owner's decision rule:** proceed to forward paper trading iff H0 holds and the mechanism verdict is not CONTRADICTED. **Paper trading here validates operations and detects gross failure; it cannot statistically confirm the edge** — at ~4 trades a year that takes decades.

## 8. Multiple testing and stop rules

DSR with N = 27 carried + 3 declared = 30, cross-trial variance 1/(n−1) (the PR-002 convention), with a sensitivity at N = 40. Descriptive tables (thresholds, SMA length, k = 1 and 5) may never be cited as the basis for adopting a variant; any new variant is a new pre-registration. A single primary endpoint (G2), so no correction within it. **The report skeleton is frozen at freeze time:** verdict; G0–G1b; G2 by era; G4; blend test; limitations; the sentence "evidence about one days-scale rule, not swing trading as a family".

## 9. Known weak points

1. Power: ~4 entries a year; E3 alone has ~26. G2 is powered by all days, but the effect lives in the tails.
2. E2 is not blind; only E3–E4 are new evidence.
3. Bands and assumed costs are judgement.
4. One instrument; QQQ shares the same upstream and may not be cited as independent confirmation.
5. The primary source for the rule is secondary; the book has not been read.
6. A second implementation written by the same author from the same spec may share the same misreading (mitigated by the manual audit).

## 10. Effort

Three sessions: data, G0 and G1 (1); G1b replication and G2 (1); G4, E4 and the report (1). Then the owner's paper-trading decision.
