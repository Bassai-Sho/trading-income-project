## 0. What changed, and how each was verified

| # | Change | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **v0.4's "zero violations" claim was a tautology, correctly called out.** *F*(ρ) := max{CAGR : ratio ≤ ρ} evaluated at any grid point's own ratio necessarily includes that point in the maximisation — zero violations is guaranteed by the definition of a running maximum, not evidence the construction is right. It verified the code, not the benchmark. Replaced with the three real checks below. |
| 2 | **The benchmark's class was genuinely incomplete — checked, not just flagged.** The frontier only maximised over *daily-rebalanced* mixes, excluding two other cheaply implementable no-skill families: **never-rebalanced** (buy once, hold — exactly the object my v0.3 "bug" had actually computed, correctly, just not the object needed at the time) and **monthly-rebalanced**. Built all three families on real E3 data (201-point daily/never grid, 41-point monthly grid — monthly requires a path-dependent loop, coarser for cost) and checked whether either alternative family ever beats the daily-rebalanced frontier at a matched ratio. **Result: it does not, at any ratio tested from 0.0 to 1.0 in steps of 0.005, except a 0.22% shift exactly at the trivial full-exposure boundary (ratio = 1.0), which is not a case any risk-reducing rule would land in.** Daily-rebalanced dominance is now a logged, checked fact for this specific window, not an assumption — and the frontier stays daily-only, per the resolution path this round's review itself proposed if the check came back this way. | Built and compared all three families directly; reported the exact maximum shift and where it occurs. |
| 3 | **The grid leak is now bounded, not just estimated.** Probed the 200 midpoints between the original 201-grid nodes (i.e., genuinely off-grid weights) on the daily-rebalanced family: **maximum leak = 0.0117%** — about 1.2 basis points, an order of magnitude below the margins this gate has been fought over, and consistent with the continuity bound estimated last round. **The pass condition now requires a strict margin of 0.05% (5bp) over the interpolated frontier**, not bare inequality — five times the measured leak, so grid resolution cannot itself decide a real outcome. |
| 4 | **Convention identity pinned** (N14): the frontier's cash leg and the rule's own cash leg must be drawn from the *same* daily DFF series under the *same* day-count convention (act/360, matching how DFF itself is quoted) — stated as one line so a mismatch can't silently shift both sides of the comparison differently. |

## 1. The dominance test, current state

**G3 (H1):** *F*(ρ) := max CAGR among a fine grid (≥201 points) of **daily-rebalanced** SPY/cash mixes with drawdown ratio ≤ ρ, on E3, using the real DFF series once pulled. **Checked and kept daily-only**, per §0 item 2 — never-rebalanced and monthly-rebalanced families were tested directly and do not beat it at any realistic ratio. The rule passes iff its realized Stage-V-net CAGR exceeds *F* at its own realized Stage-V-net drawdown ratio **by at least 5bp** (§0 item 3, the grid-leak margin). Convention identity per §0 item 4.
