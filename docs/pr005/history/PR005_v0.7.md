## 8a. Paper-trading protocol (new)

- **Purpose:** operational validation only — fill mechanics, pipeline integrity, realized slippage versus the assumed 5bp assumption. Performance is explicitly non-evidential at the rule's expected 0–1 trades/year; no conclusion about H1 may be drawn from the paper window's returns.
- **Duration:** 12 months minimum, or 2 completed round trips, whichever is later.
- **Kill criteria (operational only):** realized slippage exceeds 3× the assumed 5bp on 2 or more fills; any month where the paper-traded signal diverges from the rule's own computed output; a fill-mechanism failure.
- **Explicitly prohibited:** any performance-based continuation or kill decision during the window.
- **Forward use:** the paper window's data becomes a future out-of-sample dataset, evaluated at one pre-registered point, not continuously monitored for a verdict.
